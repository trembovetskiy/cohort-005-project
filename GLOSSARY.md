# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary fixes the words we use for what learners buy and what happens after.

## Language

### Refunds

**Refund request**:
A learner's ask to get their money back for a purchase, raised inside the Refund window. A person reviews it, and it ends approved or declined.
_Avoid_: Chargeback, Reversal, Cancellation, cancel my purchase

**Refund**:
The money given back to a learner when their Refund request is approved.
_Avoid_: Chargeback, Reversal

**Refund window**:
The fixed period after a purchase during which the learner may raise a Refund request.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cancellation window
